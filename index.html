            This resource draws on the NZ Curriculum (Toi Ririki / Visual Art, Level 3) and adapts publicly documented lesson planning for Years 5–6. Whakataukī are shared with respect as commonly used teaching kupu — kaiako are encouraged to invite local iwi, kaumātua, or mana whenua voices into this kaupapa wherever possible. Faith reflections are offered separately as this is a Christian-character school, alongside — not merged into — Māori cultural content.
